# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentBus a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, focusing on what is best for the community, and helping others
learn. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized conduct,
publishing private information, threats, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify standards and take proportionate corrective
action, including removing content or temporarily or permanently restricting
participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through
the repository's documented support channels. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
